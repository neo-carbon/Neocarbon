Questions

Answers before you ask.

Which methodologies does NeoCarbon follow?

Calculations follow the API Compendium 2021 for oil and gas sources, the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 2 and Scope 3 guidance, and the principles of ISO 14064-1 for organisation-level inventories.

Does it cover all three scopes?

Yes. Scope 1 direct emissions, Scope 2 with location-based and market-based methods, and Scope 3 across the 15 value chain categories all live in the same inventory and consolidate together.

Can I choose which GWP values are used?

You can select the IPCC assessment report used for each inventory, so results can match a regulator, a customer or your own reporting policy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. NeoCarbon is a web-based platform, so your team signs in from any modern browser and works on one central inventory, with role-based access and a full change history.

Is the methane module available?

The OGMP 2.0 module, covering reporting levels, reconciliation and Gold Standard tracking, is in development. Contact us to join the early access list.

Can NeoCarbon support a verification audit?

Each calculation stores its inputs, emission factor, GWP set and timestamp, and changes are tracked, so a verifier can follow any reported figure back to its evidence.
